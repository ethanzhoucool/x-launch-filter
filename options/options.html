Search query

X's search filters on the server and paginates natively, so what arrives is already mostly good and the gates above trim it rather than empty it. This is the sturdier setup: the algorithmic timeline is roughly 88% below any useful bar, and a page filtered to nothing is what ends infinite scroll.

The query ANDs launch phrasing with product vocabulary, and the second half is what makes it usable: "just launched" alone matches ballistic missiles, but "just launched" next to app, beta, API or SaaS does not. Tuned live, that change took the news share of a page from 3-in-5 to none, and the shipped query kept 4 of 5 posts at a 10k view floor. Keep Require launch signals on as the second line of defence.

X search has no min_views, so the view floor is converted to min_faves: using the like rate measured on a live feed (about 0.6% of views, so roughly views ÷ 170). The exact view floor and the follower ceiling are still enforced here, on the results.
